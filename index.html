Why this matters

Make the next step a safer one.

Electronic waste can contain materials that need appropriate handling, while many components can be recovered through recycling. Correct identification is a useful first step.

Image-based identification can be uncertain. Lighting, obstruction, damage and unfamiliar designs may affect results. Always use official local guidance for disposal decisions.
